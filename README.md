## `projects`

### 🚀 Kickstart Campaign Analysis
> **SQL + Python** deep-dive into **370,000+ crowdfunding campaigns** to decode what makes or breaks a Kickstarter project.

**The problem:** What separates a $500K campaign from one that never reaches its goal?

**What I uncovered:**
- 📌 Category, funding goal size, and launch timing are the strongest success predictors
- 📌 Projects with goals under $10K have significantly higher success rates than large-scale campaigns
- 📌 Weekend launches correlated with lower backer engagement

**Process:**
```
Raw CSV  ──►  Pandas Cleaning  ──►  SQL Analysis  ──►  Seaborn/Matplotlib Viz  ──►  Insights Report
```
